GoalGrow Premium

Unlimited goals. More momentum.

Premium adds unlimited active goals, a 100-point weekly bonus, and 150 additional points for every goal achieved. Start a seven-day free trial only after consenting to ₹299/month billing; cancel any time before or after the trial.

Membership

Cancellation schedules no future bill. Trial access continues until its trial end; paid access continues until the paid period ends.

Premium reward logic

Weekly: the 100-point bonus is awarded once per calendar week while Premium is active when the app next runs.
Goal completion: rewards are granted from a one-time completion flag, preventing duplicate points after reloads or repeated renders.
